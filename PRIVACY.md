# CALIBEX — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-22_

CALIBEX is a personal health-tracking application built for the sole use of its developer. It is not a commercial product and is not distributed to, or accessible by, other users.

## Data collected

CALIBEX reads health and activity data from WHOOP (via the WHOOP Developer API) for the authenticated account holder only. This includes:

- Recovery, sleep, heart-rate-variability, resting heart rate, and respiratory metrics
- Workout, strain, and heart-rate-zone data
- Cycle start/end timestamps

CALIBEX also stores user-logged data: meal photos and macros, subjective check-in responses, supplement and drink logs, and session tags.

## Where data is stored

All data is stored in a private Supabase Postgres database accessible only by the account holder. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No analytics or telemetry services are integrated.

## Data retention

Data is retained indefinitely for the purpose of longitudinal health analysis. The account holder may delete the underlying database, revoke WHOOP API access, or disconnect the integration at any time, which will halt further data collection.

## Third-party services

- **WHOOP** — source of biometric data (see [whoop.com/privacy](https://www.whoop.com/privacy))
- **Supabase** — database host (see [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy))
